# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Agent Office to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect privacy: never post other people's personal data, session logs or private paths.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors. To report a problem privately, use
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/zinkxx/agent-office/security/advisories/new) or contact the maintainer
through their GitHub profile. All reports are handled confidentially.
